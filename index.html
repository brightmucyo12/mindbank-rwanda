Frequently asked Questions

What is MindBank? +
MindBank Rwanda is an online digital preparation platform designed specifically to help candidates pass their Rwandan provisional driving license theory test on the first try through realistic practice exams and detailed tracking.
What is included in my paid subscription? +
Your subscription gives you full, unlimited access to our bank of over 300+ official provisional test questions, automated 20-question practice mock exams with real-time scoring, and performance history tracking.
How much is the subscription, and how can I pay? +
We offer two flexible plans: the Biweekly plan for 1,000 RWF and the Monthly plan for 1,500 RWF. You can pay securely and conveniently via MTN Mobile Money (MoMo) or Airtel Money directly from your registration page.
Do you offer a free trial and what is included? +
Yes! You can explore our initial practice features and get familiar with the test interface to kickstart your study sessions before upgrading to full subscription access.
