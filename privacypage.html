Welcome to iZiDrive ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our website (izidrive.site) and tell you about your privacy rights.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly to provide and improve our services, manage your subscription plan, track your mock exam results, and communicate important updates regarding your account.
